Problem Statement
Develop an intelligent AI-powered interview platform that can conduct personalized,
human-like interviews at scale. The platform should understand a candidate's
background, engage them in a natural spoken conversation, adapt its questioning
based on their responses, and evaluate their performance in a fair and consistent
manner. The mission is to make early-stage hiring faster, more objective, and more
trustworthy for recruiters, while giving candidates an accessible way to practice and
improve.
Key Features
Profile-Aware Questioning and Evaluation: The platform understands a candidate's
resume in the context of the target role and generates relevant, personalized
interview questions. At the end of the session, it produces a comprehensive
evaluation report that highlights the candidate's strengths, gaps, and overall
suitability for the role.
Conversational and Adaptive Interviewing: The interview is conducted as a natural
spoken conversation rather than a static questionnaire. The platform intelligently
adjusts the depth and direction of the interview based on each response, probing
further where the candidate shows strength and adapting where they struggle,
closely replicating the experience of a real interviewer.
Interview Integrity Monitoring: The platform observes the candidate's attention
throughout the session and identifies behaviour that may indicate the use of external
help, such as repeatedly looking away from the screen. These observations are
presented transparently to recruiters, building trust in the authenticity of every
interview.
